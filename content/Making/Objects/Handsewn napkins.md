I finished hand-sewing a set of four napkins, using 1) scraps of a chambray fabric; 2) old printed fabric from the creative reuse store; and 3) block printed cotton from Marengo Studio. Sew wrong sides together with running stitch, clip the corners, turn right-side out, and edgestitch. I really enjoyed having a hand project to take with me on a trip, and the finished napkins make me very happy indeed.
