This skirt was a lesson in patience, meaning, how not having patience led me to make mistakes and enjoy myself less. I rushed through the construction of the waistband, leaving a trail of snarly stitches in my wake, and spent several evenings in my warm apartment (the upstairs being several degrees warmer than the basement) sweating over an iron trying to get the slippery, drapey, soft fabric to stay in place long enough to sew it. Also something to be learned - the ability on a sewing machine to *go faster* means that when I'm annoyed I crank the speed up to 3x and let the machine rip, which is just when I should be slowing down. 

Pattern from [Helen's Closet](https://helensclosetpatterns.com/products/donovan-skirt) with modded pockets. 

Fabric from [Marengo Sewing Studio in Ann Arbor](https://www.marengosew.com/), a new shop that opened here that I have instantly fallen in love with.
