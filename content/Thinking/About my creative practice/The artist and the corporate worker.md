Inspired by Cortney Cassidy's article, ["Can I Be an Artist Here?"](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/can-i-be-an-artist-here/) I made my own zine comparing the way I feel as an artist who researches as part of a creative practice, and a corporate worker who performs research as a service for other people. 


Reflecting on this a few days later, I undersell the things from my corporate work that *are* genuinely useful:
- being attentive to the information needs of others
- having a clear process of how to generate and create research plans from scratch
- understanding how to design learning experiences for other people
- knowing how to deliver findings (or work, or ideas, or concepts, or art) in a way that inspires someone else to do *their* creative work
