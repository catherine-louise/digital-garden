Coined by Adam Richard Rottinghaus, in [*Upgrade Culture and Technological Change*](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/mono/10.4324/9781003193869/upgrade-culture-technological-change-adam-richard-rottinghaus).

> Upgrade culture orients individuals and organizations to react to the compulsory change of technologies, platforms and infrastructures on which communication, culture and economic practices occur... \[I]t is enacted materially through planned obsolescence, business models deploying software updates, hardware failures, or contract terms that compel upgrading
