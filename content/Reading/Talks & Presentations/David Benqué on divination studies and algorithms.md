However, there is **chicanery** involved (a chican - "a trick, a subterfuge") in AI - this idea comes via G. Cornelius
- Chicane - "artful play of semblances." Chicanes are when there is some trickery going on, some play and interpretation about what is really happening. 
- Much of the discourse around AI, the way it is positioned, even the design (e.g., ChatGPT's helpful bot interface) is a form of **insincere chicanery**
- Look at "the power relationship between diviner and client" - is it equal? Or is the diviner pretending to have more knowledge than the client, suggesting they are the only person who can have access to the knowledge? ChatGPT is insincere, as are its boosters; they suggest they are the only people with access to the divine knowledge of ChatGPT
- "Divinatory performance"

Consider data science as the whole. What does it do?
- The data science loop "extracts data from the world, [[Vectoral politics|vectorizes it]], feeds into a machine learning service, and computes a prediction—this is, in my view, a giant chican. It is a prop in a divinatory performance that is insincere because it is not framed as being a chican at all. Data is just framed a source of truth, so-called AI is an unbiased source of knowledge, etc."
